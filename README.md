## Method note

Figures are sourced to published industry, government, and trade press reporting
current as of September 2026. Currency conversions use approximate mid-2026 rates
(USD/MXN ≈ 17.6, USD/EUR ≈ 0.86, USD/CAD ≈ 1.385) and are marked as indicative.
Cross-border price comparisons carry tax-treatment and vehicle-segment caveats,
which are stated explicitly wherever a comparison is drawn.
